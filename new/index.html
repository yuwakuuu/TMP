    Questions, answered.

    What does “starting at” mean?

    It's the price for a focused, well-built project. Bigger projects with more pages, features or connections cost more. You'll always get a clear quote before any work begins.

    What is TMP+?

    TMP+ is our website membership, and it comes in two sizes. TMP+ Care is $99.99 a month: we cover your domain cost and upkeep, keep your site maintained 24/7, and make updates for free whenever you need them, within reason. TMP+ Grow is $249.99 a month: everything in Care, plus we set up and manage your Google Business Profile, handle your local SEO and website SEO, and send you a simple monthly report. Both are optional. Your website is yours either way.

    Do you do SEO and Google Business Profiles?

    Yes, through TMP+ Grow. For $249.99 a month we set up and manage your Google Business Profile so you show up on Google Maps, and we handle your local SEO and website SEO to improve your chances of landing on Google's front page. The goal is simple: when people nearby search for what you do, they find you.

    Can you replace software I already pay for?

    Often, yes. We built Total Quality Management a desktop app that replaced QuickBooks and saves them $2,000 a year. Tell us what you use and what it costs, and we'll tell you if a custom system makes sense.

    I already have a website. Can you help?

    Yes. We can redesign it, rebuild it or improve what's there, and help more customers find it.

    How long does a project take?

    It depends on the scope. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote, before anything starts.

    What kinds of businesses do you work with?

    Small and local businesses of every kind: restaurants, salons, contractors, cleaning companies, pet care, shops, studios and more.
