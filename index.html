#4SAMMY

SAM: Suicide Awareness Movement

SAM is our project in Sammy’s name. Its message is simple: please check on those around you. A text, a call or a visit can mean more than you know.

If you’re struggling, you’re not alone.

In the US, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time, day or night. You can also chat at 988lifeline.org. Outside the US, find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does “starting at” mean?

It’s the price for a focused, well-built project. Larger projects with more pages, features or integrations cost more. You’ll always get a fixed quote before any work begins.

Do I need the membership?

No. It’s completely optional. Your project is yours either way. The membership is for owners who want updates, new features and tech support on call for $60 a month.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can redesign it, rebuild it or simply improve what’s there, and help more customers find it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. You’ll get a clear timeline along with your quote, before anything starts.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small and local businesses of every kind: restaurants, salons, contractors, cleaning companies, pet care, shops, studios and more.
