Sea of Cortez · Sonora, Mexico

San Carlos

Located on the Sea of Cortez, the lovely Mexican resort community of San Carlos, Sonora offers something for everyone. For water sports enthusiasts, there are beautiful sand beaches, spectacular diving and snorkeling, kayaking and offshore fishing.

San Carlos is only a 250 mile, 4-hour drive from Nogales, AZ on Mexico Highway 15, a four-lane divided highway.

Quintana Roo, Mexico

Cozumel

Come dive the crystal clear water of Cozumel where the visibility seldom drops below 100 feet. Drift diving is the plan — drift the outer walls of Palancar, Santa Rosa and more, or dive the more leisurely inner reefs such as Yucab and Cedral.

In our book, Cozumel is the best dive value around. We prefer Dressel Divers for their consistent quality, fast boats, variety of locations and just plain friendliness.

Ecuador · UNESCO World Heritage

Galápagos

There’s a reason why Galápagos liveaboard diving is on every diver’s bucket list. A paradise of protected islands bursting with marine life found nowhere else on earth — aggregating hammerheads and whale sharks, penguins, marine iguanas and more. At isolated Wolf and Darwin, huge schools of scalloped hammerheads, silky sharks and tuna float on by.

Exuma Cays · All Star Aqua Cat

Bahamas

Experience exceptional scuba diving like no other place in the Atlantic or Caribbean. Because the Exuma Cays can only be reached by liveaboard, this underwater world is pristine — colourful fish, macro-critters, corals, sponges, fans, lobster and sharks.

Passive shark feeding dives, walls that begin as shallow as 40 feet, high-speed drifts through the cuts, and blue holes banked by patch reefs with huge schools of fish. Voted best liveaboard in the Caribbean by Scuba Diving Magazine readers, year after year.

Heart of the Maldives · Spirit of Maldives

Maldives

Renowned for encounters with majestic pelagics — swim alongside manta rays, whale sharks, turtles and eagle rays. Seven nights through the central atolls: shark dives, coral gardens, manta cleaning stations, blue and yellow soft-coral caves, and night dives where we often meet sharks and mantas.

Itinerary: North Malé Atoll · Rasdhu · Ari · Felidhu · South Malé.
