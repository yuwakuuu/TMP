About The Scuba Shop

Founded in 2008.
Still diving every month.

The Scuba Shop is a PADI 5-Star Instructor Development Center dedicated to providing outstanding service to the scuba diving community in Arizona and beyond.

With more than 50 years of combined experience, our team has built a trusted reputation for excellence in dive education, safety and customer care. As one of Arizona’s premier dive centers, we proudly serve both recreational and professional divers, offering comprehensive training, equipment and travel services designed to inspire adventure and confidence in every diver.
