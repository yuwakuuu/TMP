About Us

A family business, built on quality.

Total Quality Management is a family-owned commercial cleaning company in Mesa, Arizona.

Clean spaces. Lasting relationships.

We focus on commercial cleaning: keeping offices, facilities and commercial properties clean and presentable for the people who work in and visit them.

Quality isn't just part of our name — it's how we approach every job. We believe good cleaning comes down to consistency, care and communication, and that the best customer relationships are the ones that last for years.

As a family-owned business, we're not a faceless corporation. When you call, you reach people who take personal pride in the work and in the reputation that comes with it.

Our Story

Since 1985.

Total Quality Management was established in 1985 and has been keeping commercial spaces clean for decades.

Over the years we have worked with well-known companies, including Toys “R” Us and many more.

We also work as a subcontractor, partnering with other contractors and facility service companies to deliver quality cleaning and floor care.

What We Stand For

The standards behind every job.

  • Quality

    Quality is in our name. We hold every job to a clear, consistent standard.

  • Reliability

    We show up when we say we will and do what we agreed to do — so cleaning is one less thing you need to manage.

  • Professionalism

    Respect for your property, your people and your schedule, with straightforward communication from start to finish.

  • Attention to Detail

    Corners, edges, baseboards and fixtures — the details people notice are the ones we pay attention to.
