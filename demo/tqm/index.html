FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long has Total Quality Management been in business?

TQM was established in 1985 and is family-owned. We’ve been keeping commercial spaces clean for decades.

What kinds of facilities do you clean?

Commercial spaces — including grocery and retail stores, medical and donation centers, offices and commercial properties. If you’re not sure your space is a fit, just ask.

Do you take care of hard floors?

Yes. Floor care is a big part of our work, from polished concrete in busy grocery aisles to high-gloss vinyl tile. You can see real examples in our project gallery.

Do you work as a subcontractor?

Yes. We partner with other contractors and facility service companies to deliver quality cleaning and floor care for their clients.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Mesa, Arizona and serve Mesa, Arizona and surrounding communities. Not sure we cover your location? Give us a call.
