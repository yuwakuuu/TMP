One memory

Every brain gets TOB's persona, your long-term memories and the recent conversation. A project carries on whichever brain is answering.

A team, not a queue

A brain can ask another for a second opinion mid-task. They also teach each other through a shared notebook of lessons that every brain reads.

Your order

Voice & face

Press Ctrl+Alt+Space anywhere, even in a game, and speak. He replies in a warm, old-butler voice. Say “Tob” while he's talking and he stops mid-sentence to listen. Recognition, speech and the wake word all run locally on your PC.

Computer control

Your laptop is
his body.

TOB doesn't just talk about doing things. He launches apps and projects, manages windows, files and processes, sets volume and brightness, and can see and drive the screen with the mouse and keyboard.

While he's controlling the screen, a banner stays on top. Shove the mouse into the top-left corner, or press Ctrl+Alt+X, and he stops instantly.

His main job

A Minecraft dev server that never gets lost.

TOB runs the KingdomRP dev server. He always knows whether it's up and who's online. He finds it by its port whoever started it, sends console commands over RCON, and stops it gracefully so the world saves. Quit TOB and the server keeps running. When he comes back, he reconnects to it.

Safety & privacy

Powerful.
With guard rails.

Speech stays home

Synthesis, recognition and the wake word run locally. Wake-word clips are checked and discarded, never stored.

No permanent deletes

Deleting goes to the Recycle Bin. System folders and TOB's own secrets are off-limits.

Always asks

PowerShell commands and shutdown or restart show an approval card every time, with the exact command.

Locked to TOB's tools

Each brain's own shell and file tools are switched off. TOB's checked, approved actions are the only way any brain can act.

Honest by design

TOB never claims to have done something he didn't do.

Light on your PC

About 0.04% CPU while hidden in the tray and around 1% when visible and idle. The orb renders on the GPU.
