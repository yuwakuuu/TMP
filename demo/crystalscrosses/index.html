    Meet Linda

    Linda Huss, known to her clients as The Coach, started her spiritual journey at three years old. A lifelong love of animals and music showed her early on where beauty and inner peace come from.

    Her mom taught her to stay positive, believe in herself and take chances. That can-do attitude is what she brings to every visit. She opened Crystals & Crosses to share good energy with Apache Junction and the towns around it, and she has a special soft spot for people who are brand new to crystals.

    Whether you come in for a stone or a session, Linda will listen first, then help you set a goal and take real steps toward it.
