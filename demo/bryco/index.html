What we cut

Cold milling for every stage of a paving job, from a light surface grind to tearing out failed pavement, plus the trucking and sweeping that clean up after it.

  • Mill and overlay prep

    We grind the worn top layer to an even depth so the new lift bonds tight and still meets curbs, gutters, and drains at the right grade.

  • Full-depth removal

    Failed pavement comes out down to base for reconstruction, loaded straight into trucks as we go.

  • Tie-ins and keyways

    Clean butt joints at driveways, curbs, valley gutters, and paving limits, so new asphalt meets old flush instead of feathering out.

  • Grade and drainage correction

    We cut down high spots and restore curb reveal to fix ponding and send water where it belongs.

  • Parking lots and streets

    Commercial lots, HOA streets, and school campuses, phased so the property can stay open while we work.

  • Trucking and haul-off

    Our own trucks keep the mill moving and the jobsite clear of millings.

  • Sweeping

    Our sweepers clean up behind the mill so tack and the new lift bond tight, and we sweep streets, parking lots and job sites too.
